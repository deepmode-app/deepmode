Contact

Deepmode is built for people who care about their work. If something is unclear, broken or missing, we want to hear from you.

The fastest way to reach us is by email:

hi@deepmode.app

We read every message. Typical use cases:

  • product questions or feedback,
  • bug reports (ideally with steps or screenshots),
  • billing or account issues,
  • partnership or integration ideas.

We aim to respond within 24–48 hours on weekdays. If your request involves sensitive account changes, we may ask you to verify ownership via the email linked to your Deepmode account.

For data or privacy requests, please mention “Privacy” in the subject line.
