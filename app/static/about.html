About Deepmode

A premium deep work system for people who take their time seriously.

Why Deepmode exists

Time is the most expensive resource you have. Once spent, it doesn't come back.

Most people lose hours each day to scattered work and digital distraction. They sit down to focus, but the pull of notifications, tabs, and context-switching erodes their attention before any real progress happens.

Deepmode exists to give you structure, momentum, and insight. It's a system for protecting your time and building the discipline of focused work — one block at a time.

How Deepmode works

Deepmode is designed as a gym for your focus:

  • Install the Chrome extension and block the sites that usually derail you
  • Start a timed deep work session — 5, 25, 50, or 90 minutes
  • Name your task and project so every block has clear intent
  • Track your streaks, completion rate, and discipline score in the dashboard
  • Review AI-generated insights each week to spot patterns and improve

Who Deepmode is for

Deepmode is built for professionals, students, founders, researchers, and builders — anyone whose work depends on long, uninterrupted focus.

If you treat your time like capital, and you're serious about what you're building, Deepmode is designed for you.

Our philosophy

  • Small wins compound. One block a day builds the identity of a focused person.
  • Momentum beats motivation. Show up daily and let the streak do the work.
  • Discipline is freedom. The more you protect your time, the more you can do with it.
  • AI insights to help you get slightly better each week — not to replace your judgment.
