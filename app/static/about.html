About Deepmode

A premium deepwork system for high-performance professionals.

Deepmode is built for engineers, founders, analysts, and high-performers who treat focus as a skill. We provide distraction blocking, session tracking, and progress insights to help you build consistent deep work habits.

Our Mission

To help you protect your focus, track your discipline, and build your streaks. Deepmode is more than a tool—it's a system for developing the identity of a focused person.

How It Works

Install the Chrome extension, start a deepwork session, and let Deepmode block distracting websites. Track your progress, see your discipline score, and watch your streaks grow. One block a day builds the identity of a focused person.

Deepmode is designed for those who take their work seriously. We offer session tracking, detailed analytics, progress reports, and streak insights to help you maintain consistent focus habits.
