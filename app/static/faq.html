FAQ

Straight answers to common questions about Deepmode.

What is Deepmode? +

Deepmode is a focus system that helps you work in timed blocks, track your streaks and discipline, and block distracting websites. It's built for professionals and students who need long, uninterrupted focus.

How does the Chrome extension work? +

Install the extension, configure which sites you want blocked during focus time, specify the task you want to focus on, then start a session of desired time. While your timer runs, those sites are inaccessible, with complete control over your browser. When the session ends, access returns to normal and your session is recorded in your dashboard.

What are deepwork sessions? +

A deepwork session is a timed block of focused work — 5, 25, 50, or 90 minutes. You name your task, start the timer, and commit to staying with it until the end. Each completed session builds your streak and discipline score.

What does the AI actually analyze? +

The AI reviews your session data — minutes worked, completion rates, streaks, projects, and categories. It identifies patterns in your behaviour and gives you actionable insights to improve. It does not analyze screen content, keystrokes, or what you're working on inside your browser.

Do I need an account to start? +

No. You can try Deepmode in guest mode without signing up. Create a free account when you want to save your sessions and sync progress to your personal dashboard.

What's included in the free version? +

Free includes up to 3 sessions per day, 5 and 25-minute timers, project tagging, and a dashboard with streaks and discipline score. It's enough to build the habit.

What's included in Pro? +

Pro unlocks unlimited sessions, 50 and 90-minute deep blocks, full session history, AI-generated daily and weekly insights, and advanced analytics. It's for people who are serious about compounding their focus.

Does Deepmode block all distractions? +

Deepmode blocks the websites you configure as distracting during active sessions. It won't block desktop apps or mobile — it's focused on browser-based distraction.

Which browsers are supported? +

Deepmode works on all Chromium-based desktop browsers — Chrome, Edge, Brave, Arc, and others. Firefox and Safari support is not currently available.

Does Deepmode store my data? +

Yes, we store your session data (times, tasks, projects, categories) so you can see your history and streaks. We do not store page contents, keystrokes, or screenshots. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Can I cancel anytime? +

Yes. You can cancel your Pro subscription anytime from the Stripe billing portal. You'll keep Pro access until the end of your current billing period.

Is there a mobile app? +

Not yet. Deepmode is currently focused on desktop — where most serious work happens. Mobile support is on the roadmap.

Is Deepmode safe for work computers? +

Yes. The extension only reads the URL of your current tab to check if it should be blocked. It doesn't capture screen content, send browsing history, or access sensitive data. It's safe for personal and work machines.

I found a bug or have a feature idea. +

We want to hear it. Email hi@deepmode.app with details. Deepmode is built for people who use it daily — your feedback shapes the product.
