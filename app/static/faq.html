FAQ

Straight answers to the most common questions about Deepmode.

What is Deepmode, in one line? +

Deepmode is a focus tool that locks you into timed work blocks, tracks your streaks and discipline, and blocks the websites that usually derail you.

How do the focus blocks work? +

You choose a task, project and duration (for example 25, 50 or 90 minutes). When you start a block, the timer runs and your session is recorded in the dashboard. If you’re using the Chrome extension, your distraction sites are blocked for the duration of the block.

What’s the difference between Free and Pro? +

The free tier lets you run up to 3 sessions per day with the core Deepmode flows. Pro unlocks unlimited sessions, longer deep blocks, richer stats and future power-user features.

Does Deepmode track everything I do in the browser? +

No. The extension only needs to know which site you’re on in order to block distraction sites. We do not record page contents, keystrokes or screenshots. For details, see the Privacy Policy.

What happens if I forget to end a session? +

Deepmode will automatically close obviously stale sessions after a long period of inactivity so they don’t distort your stats. You can always clean up or edit sessions from your dashboard.

Who is Deepmode for? +

Deepmode is used by students, engineers, founders and independent creators who want a quiet, intentional way to ship work without turning their life into a to-do app.

Is there a mobile app? +

Today, Deepmode is focused on laptop/desktop work with the web app and Chrome extension. Mobile support is on the roadmap once the core experience is battle-tested.

How do I cancel Pro? +

You can cancel future renewals at any time via the billing portal link you receive from Stripe, or by emailing hi@deepmode.app. Your current paid period will continue until it expires.

Can I delete my account and data? +

Yes. Email hi@deepmode.app from the address you registered with and request deletion. We will remove or anonymise your personal data unless we are required to retain some information for legal or accounting reasons.

I found a bug or have a feature idea. +

We want to hear it. Send a short note (ideally with steps to reproduce or a screenshot) to hi@deepmode.app. The product is built for people who actually use it every day.
